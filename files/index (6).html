Our Story

Rooted in Nabeul's clay and citrus

Nabeul is a town of potters and orange groves, known for hand-painted ceramics and orange-blossom water distilled every spring. Garden Coffee draws on that heritage: a garden shaded by fruit trees, cups thrown by local artisans, and a menu that treats coffee with the same patience the town gives its clay.


Whether you're meeting friends, working quietly with a laptop, or simply watching the light move through the leaves, this is a place built to slow you down.
